# Brand and project positioning
Official name (EN): Skivira Voice Dispatcher
Official name (RU): Skivira Voice Dispatcher
Subtitle (EN): AI voice dispatcher for inbound calls and orders
Subtitle (RU): AI voice dispatcher for inbound calls and orders
Abbreviation/slug: SVD / skivira-voice-dispatcher
Note: "Voice" is broader than "Call", so we maintain flexibility for WhatsApp/web-calls/chat.


1) Short overview
Goal: to create a voice dispatcher that takes inbound calls, answers product questions, places orders, fills out spreadsheets, sends confirmations, and transfers to a live operator if necessary. We start with a limited set of scenarios and expand functionality in layers.

2) What is already real in 2025
- Live conversation with interruptions and pauses, natural clarifications.
- Understanding of free speech with accents and noise at a call center level.
- Function calls from dialogue: checking price and availability, creating an order, writing to a spreadsheet, sending SMS and e-mail.
- Call management: hold, transfer, callback.

3) Architecture
Telephony
- SIP or a provider like Twilio or Plivo. Preferably a media stream via WebSocket to the backend.

Real-time pipeline
- STT — streaming speech recognition. Partial hypotheses every 200–400 ms.
- Pause and interruption detector. The bot goes silent when the client can be heard.
- LLM with tools. The model does not fantasize, but calls functions.
- TTS with streaming. The voice starts speaking quickly and can stop instantly upon interruption.

Integrations
- Google Sheets or a database for recording orders.
- Website or CRM for assortment and prices.
- Address and ZIP code validation.
- SMS and e-mail for confirmations.

Conversation state
- A single CallState with explicit slots and flags. Example:
{
  "caller": {"phone": "", "name": "", "locale": "ru-RU"},
  "intent": "create_order",
  "slots": {"product": "", "qty": 1, "address": "", "datetime": ""},
  "cart": [{"sku": "", "name": "", "qty": 1, "price": 0}],
  "meta": {"consent_recording": true, "handoff": false}
}

4) Key challenges and how to beat them
- Latency. Aim for a response in 1–2 seconds. Use a two-way stream and short phrases.
- Complex entities: addresses, names, SKUs. Spelling out mode. NATO alphabet or common names for letters can be used.
- Clear confirmations. Before creating an order, repeat all fields in one block and ask for confirmation.
- Talkative clients. Summary. Briefly retell and ask if correct.
- Payment. Do not dictate cards to the bot. Either a secure IVR with a number pad or a secure link via SMS.
- Recording and the law. California requires the consent of both parties. A phrase about recording and its purpose at the beginning. If refused, turn off recording.
- Escalation. After several unsuccessful attempts or intent conflicts, transfer to a human.

5) MVP area of responsibility
- Inbound calls. 5–10 typical tasks: talk about the product, check availability, place an order, take address and time, send SMS and e-mail, transfer to operator.
- Payment by link. Outbound calls and complex scenarios will be added later.

Strict AI rules
- Short replicas. One thought at a time.
- Always confirm before saving an order.
- Does not invent. If there is no data, clarifies or transfers to a human.
- Any structure first into CallState, then confirmation, then function call.

6) Mini-spec of functions for LLM
[
  {"name":"get_product_info","args":{"query":"string"}},
  {"name":"check_inventory","args":{"sku":"string","qty":"number"}},
  {"name":"create_order","args":{"customer":{"name":"string","phone":"string","address":"string"},"items":[{"sku":"string","qty":"number"}],"delivery_time":"string"}},
  {"name":"append_to_sheet","args":{"row":"object"}},
  {"name":"send_sms","args":{"to":"string","text":"string"}},
  {"name":"handoff_to_human","args":{"reason":"string"}}
]

7) Example of a dialogue with interruption
Client: Hello. Do you have AquaPro water filters, three pieces...
Bot: Yes. Let me clarify the model. Do you need the AP-100 or AP-200 series
Client interrupts: AP-200, and deliver to...
Bot goes silent and listens. Then: We have AP-200. Three pieces. Delivery today by six is possible. Tell me the address
Client dictates the address.
Bot: Repeating. Three AP-200s. Address Ocean Avenue 123, Santa Monica. Deliver by six. Do you confirm
Client: Yes
Actions: create_order, write to spreadsheet, SMS with order number.

8) Quality and control
- Metrics. Share of tasks without human intervention. Average processing time. Number of repeats and escalations.
- Test sets. Accents, noise, half-heard addresses, similar SKUs, numbers, and dates.
- Log. Transcript, function calls, final records. We store personal data only when necessary and within time limits.

9) Security and privacy
- Card numbers and CVV do not pass through LLM. Either IVR or payment link.
- Call recording only after explicit consent. We inform about the storage location and deletion method.
- If the domain is medicine or finance, narrow scenarios and legal consent text are mandatory.

10) What to choose immediately
- Telephony provider.
- A pair of STT and TTS for quality and speed.
- Where to store product knowledge. Spreadsheet, database, or search index.
- List of first intents and slots.
- Texts for greeting and recording disclaimer.

11) Scenario outline: Product Order
Step 1. Greeting. Message about recording and how the assistant can help.
Step 2. Identifying intent. Place an order, check price, clarify delivery.
Step 3. Collecting entities. Product, quantity, address, time, contact details.
Step 4. Confirmation in one block.
Step 5. Call create_order, write to spreadsheet, send SMS and e-mail with order number and payment link.
Step 6. Closing. Gratitude and instructions on how to contact if changes arise.

Notes
- We build in layers. We start with simple scenarios and bring them to metric stability.
- The goal of the first release is stable answers and correct recording of orders into the spreadsheet.
