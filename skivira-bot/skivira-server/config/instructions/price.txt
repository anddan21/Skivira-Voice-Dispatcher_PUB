Service Pricing Policy
----------------------

The service call fee, which includes diagnostics, is $95.
If you agree to proceed with the repairs at the price quoted by the technician, the $95 service call fee will be waived (you only pay for the repair).
The final repair price is determined by the technician and is based on the cost of parts, required labor, and applicable taxes.
---

Payment Policy
--------------

Payment is due to the technician on-site upon completion of the service.

Accepted Methods:
- Cash
- Personal Checks
- Credit cards

Please note, we do not accept cryptocurrencies (such as Bitcoin).
